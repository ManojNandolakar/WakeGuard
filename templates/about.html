About Our AI Driver Monitoring System

Driving safely goes beyond staying awake. Our AI-powered system monitors multiple indicators in real time—detecting drowsiness, distracted behavior, and potential hazards—so you stay alert and in control on every journey.

Eye Closure Detection (EAR)

Monitors blink rate and eye closure to identify signs of fatigue before they become dangerous.

Yawn Detection (MAR)

Detects yawns to catch early signs of tiredness, alerting drivers instantly.

Head Tilt Monitoring

Tracks abnormal head movements, like nodding or tilting, that indicate drowsiness or inattention.

Phone Usage Detection

Recognizes when the driver is using a phone, helping reduce distracted driving incidents.

Face Not Detected Alerts

Ensures the driver’s face is in frame; alerts trigger if the driver is slouched, looking away, or absent.

Real-Time Monitoring

Our system tracks eye closure, yawning, and head movements in real time, ensuring drivers get immediate alerts when fatigue is detected.

AI-Powered Accuracy

Using advanced computer vision algorithms, our AI distinguishes between normal behavior and dangerous drowsiness patterns with high reliability.

Instant Alerts

Audible and visual notifications keep drivers aware, helping them take timely breaks and avoid accidents.

User-Friendly Dashboard

All features, live video feeds, and monitoring tools are accessible from an intuitive dashboard designed for quick insights.
