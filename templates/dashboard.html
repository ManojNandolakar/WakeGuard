AI Driver Drowsiness Detection

  • Real-Time Monitoring – Tracks the driver’s eyes, face, and head movements continuously using the camera.
  • Fatigue Detection – Detects drowsiness using Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR).
  • Instant Alerts – Triggers an alarm immediately when signs of fatigue are detected.
  • AI-Powered Analysis – Uses computer vision algorithms to analyze driver behavior accurately.
  • Accident Prevention – Helps reduce the risk of road accidents caused by driver fatigue.
  • Live Dashboard – Displays real-time monitoring and system status directly on the dashboard.
